# Searching the CRM (Ctrl + K)

Press **Ctrl + K** (on a Mac, **⌘ + K**), or click **Search anything…** at the top of any page. On a phone, tap the **search icon** in the
header. Type a few letters:

* **Pages and features**, from your own menu: "att" finds Attendance, "incent" finds Sales Incentives, "emp" finds Team Members.
* **People**, by name, e-mail, designation, department or phone number.
* **Customers and leads**, by business or contact name, customer ID (CUS-…), phone number or e-mail.
* **Meetings**, by title, reference, type or date; **support tickets** by number or subject; **departments**; **notices**.

You only ever see what you can already open in your own dashboard.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| ↑ / ↓ | move through the results |
| Enter | open the highlighted result |
| Esc | close search (the cursor goes back where it was) |
| Tab / Shift + Tab | move between the field and the buttons |

## What opens

* **A page** opens in your dashboard.
* **A meeting** or **notice** opens on its own page.
* **A person, customer, lead, ticket or department** opens in a panel on the right (full screen on a phone). The panel has its own
  address: the browser's **Back** button closes it, and the link can be bookmarked or shared with someone who has access. Where your
  dashboard has a matching page, **Go to …** opens it.
* If a record was removed, or you no longer have access to it, the panel says so.

## When the field is empty

You see **Recently visited** (your last pages and records) and **Quick navigation**.
* Remove one entry with its × button, or press **Clear recent history** to empty the list.
* Recent items are kept on this device for you only; someone else signing in sees none of them.

## Messages you may see

* **No results found**: try another name, page, customer or feature.
* **Unable to complete your search**: press **Try again**. Pages can still be found meanwhile.
* **Your session has ended. Sign in again to search.**
