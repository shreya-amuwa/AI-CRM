**Raise an Issue** is one page in your dashboard menu. You use it to tell HR about a problem, or to ask HR for a meeting. Team Members, Team Leads and Department Heads all use the same page.

## Before you start

:::prereq
- **Raise an Issue** is in the **Team** section of the menu, next to **Chat** (on a phone, open the menu with the menu button first).
- You do not type who you are. Your **name, department and designation** are read from your profile and shown at the top of the page. The date and time are recorded when you submit.
:::

## Steps

1. Open **Raise an Issue**.
2. In the box at the top, choose **Raise an Issue** or **Meeting Request**. Both forms are on this same page.
3. For an issue, fill in the **Issue Title**, **Issue Category**, **Priority** and the **Detailed Issue Description**. You may add a **Preferred Resolution or Additional Comments** and one or more **attachments** (up to 5 files, 5 MB each: images, PDF, text, CSV, Word or Excel).
4. For a meeting, fill in the **Meeting Subject**, **Meeting Category**, **Preferred Meeting Date**, **Preferred Time**, **Meeting Mode** (In Person or Online) and the **Purpose**. **Additional Notes** are optional.
5. Press **Submit Issue** or **Submit Meeting Request**. You get a **reference number** (for example `ISS-2026-00001` or `MTG-2026-00001`). Quote it if you speak to HR.

:::note A meeting request is not a meeting
HR reviews your request and confirms the real date and time. Until then it shows as **Pending**.
:::

## Following HR's response

Below the form, **My issues** or **My meeting requests** lists everything you sent, with its status. Open one to see:

- the status (**New**, **In Progress**, **Waiting for your reply**, **Resolved**, **Closed**) and who handles it;
- **HR's response**, and the history of what happened;
- for a meeting: the **confirmed date, time, mode, location or online link** and any instructions, or the **reason** if HR declined, rescheduled or cancelled it.

If HR needs more information, the issue shows **Waiting for your reply**. Write your reply (and attach files if needed) in the issue and press **Send reply**. You can also reply to an issue that was marked resolved; it goes back to HR.

If HR proposes **another time** for a meeting, choose **Accept this time** or **I cannot make it**. You can **Withdraw request** or **Cancel this meeting** (you are asked to confirm).

You are notified in the **Notification Center** when HR responds, changes the status, assigns your issue or schedules your meeting. Click the notification to open the issue.

## What you should see

:::result
- After submitting, a green confirmation with your reference number, and the issue or request at the top of your list.
- Only **your own** issues and meetings. Nobody else in your team or department can open them. HR can see all of them.
- HR's internal notes are never shown to you.
:::

## If something goes wrong

- **"Enter an issue title" / "Choose an issue category"**: the form tells you what is missing; nothing is sent until it is complete.
- **A file is refused**: check the type and the 5 MB limit, and that the file is a real PDF/image (a renamed file is refused).
- **Submitted twice by mistake**: you get the same reference number, not a second issue.
- **"This issue is closed"**: raise a new issue.
- HR staff work the cases from their own dashboard; HR members do not have this page.
