# Chatting with Your Amuwa Team

The **Chat** tab is a quick way to talk to the two people who look after your account: your Team Member and your Team Lead. You all share one conversation, so either of them can answer you.

## Open the chat

1. Sign in to your dashboard.
2. Click **Chat** in the menu (under **Activity**). On a phone, tap **Live chat** at the bottom of the screen.
3. The top of the chat names your team, for example "Your Amuwa team", with your Team Member and your Team Lead.

## Send a message

1. Type in the box that says "Write a message".
2. Press kbd:Enter to send. Press kbd:Shift+Enter to start a new line.
3. Your messages appear on the right. Replies from your team appear on the left with their name and whether they are your **Team Member** or **Team Lead**.

## Send a file

1. Click the paperclip.
2. Choose up to 5 files. Each file can be up to 5 MB and can be an image (PNG, JPEG or WEBP), a PDF, a text file, a CSV file, a Word document or an Excel sheet.
3. Wait until the file shows as attached, add a message if you like, and send.

To open a file someone sent you, click its name. Files are private to you and your team.

## What you should see

:::result
- Two blue ticks under your message mean your team has read it.
- New replies appear without refreshing the page. A number next to **Chat** in the menu shows how many replies you have not read yet, and you also get one entry in your **Notification Center** for new replies.
:::

## Good to know

:::note When your team is not assigned yet
If no Team Member has been assigned to you yet, you can read the chat but not write. A note says "Your team has not been assigned yet. You will be able to write as soon as a Team Member is assigned." You will be able to write as soon as one is assigned.
:::

:::note If your team changes
If your Team Member or Team Lead changes, the chat stays and a line appears saying "Your assigned team was updated", with the new names.
:::

- **If a message does not send**, it stays on screen marked **Not sent**. Click **Retry**. It is never sent twice.
- **Earlier messages**: click **Load earlier messages** at the top of a long chat.
- **Who can see the chat**: you, your Team Member and your Team Lead. The head of their department can also read it to check the quality of our service, but cannot write in it. No other customer can see it.
- **Use a ticket for formal requests.** For problems you want tracked until they are solved, raise a ticket: see [Raising support tickets](/client/raising-support-tickets).
- Do not send passwords or card numbers in chat.

## If something goes wrong

- If you cannot send, check your internet connection and click **Retry**.
- If the file is refused, check its type and that it is under 5 MB.
