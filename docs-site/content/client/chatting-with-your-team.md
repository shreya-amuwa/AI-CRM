The **Chat with Team** page gives you a direct, real-time conversation with the people who look after your account — your assigned **Team Member** and **Team Lead**. Everyone shares one thread so either of them can reply right away.

## Before you start

:::prereq
- You are signed in to your Client Dashboard at `/client/login`.
- An account **Team Member** has been assigned to your business.
:::

## Opening Chat with Team

1. Click **Chat with Team** in the left navigation menu (or tap the chat shortcut on mobile).
2. At the top of the chat window, you will see the names of your assigned **Team Member** and **Team Lead**.

## Sending a message

1. Click the message box labeled **Write a message**.
2. Type your message and press `kbd:Enter` (or click **Send**) to send it. Press `kbd:Shift+Enter` to add a new line.
3. Your messages appear on the right side of the conversation; replies from your team appear on the left with the sender's name and role badge (**Team Member** or **Team Lead**).

## Sending file attachments

1. Click the paperclip button next to the message box.
2. Select up to **5 files** per message, up to **5 MB** per file. Supported file types include images (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.webp`), PDFs (`.pdf`), text (`.txt`), CSV (`.csv`), Word documents, and Excel sheets.
3. Wait for the attachment preview to finish loading, add an optional note, and send.
4. Click any file in the chat thread to open or download it.

## What you should see

:::result
- Two blue ticks under your message indicate that your team has read it.
- New replies appear automatically in real time without refreshing the page, and unread message counts appear next to **Chat with Team** in the sidebar and in your **Notification Center**.
:::

## Good to know

:::note When your team is being assigned
If your account has just been created and a Team Member has not been assigned yet, the chat displays a notice explaining that you will be able to write as soon as a Team Member is assigned.
:::

:::tip When to use Chat vs Support Tickets
Use **Chat with Team** for quick questions, day-to-day coordination, and sharing files. When you have a technical or billing issue that you want formally tracked with a ticket reference number until resolved, open a request in [Support Tickets](/client/raising-support-tickets).
:::

- **Retrying a failed message**: If your internet connection drops while sending, the message stays visible marked **Not sent**. Click **Retry** to send it without creating a duplicate.
- **Loading older messages**: Scroll to the top of a long conversation and click **Load earlier messages** to view older history.
- **Keep credentials safe**: Never share passwords or sensitive banking PINs in chat.
