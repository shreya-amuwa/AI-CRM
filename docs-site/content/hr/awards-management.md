**Award Management** in the HR dashboard has three entries: **Birthdays**, **Anniversaries** and **Awards**. **Awards** holds the seven monthly awards: Professionalism Excellence of the Month, Rising Star of the Month, Player of the Month, Emerging Leader of the Month, Department of the Month, Team of the Month and Business Icon of the Month. Only HR staff and the Super Admin can open it; the system refuses everyone else.

## Choose the month

1. Open **Award Management**, then **Awards**.
2. Choose the month and year with the arrows or the month field. **Award history** lists the months that already have awards.
3. Each award card shows the month, the number of nominees and winners, and its status. Press **Manage Award**.

Every month is kept separately. Adding nominees in a new month never changes an earlier month.

## Add nominees

1. Press **Add Nominee**.
2. Search for and choose the employee (for Team of the Month, the team; for Department of the Month, the department). The department, team and designation are filled in from the CRM.
3. Enter the reason for the nomination and, if you like, supporting remarks. Check the award month.
4. Press **Add nominee**, or **Save & add another** to add the next one. You can add as many nominees as you need.

The same person, team or department cannot be nominated twice for the same award and month. Use the pencil to edit the reason and remarks, and the bin to remove a nominee.

## Declare winners

1. Press **Declare Winner** (or the trophy on a nominee's row).
2. Tick one nominee, or several. Add winner remarks if you like, and confirm.

Winners appear in the gold **Winners** section, each separately, with the declaration date and remarks. To withdraw a declaration, press **Revoke** and give the reason; the declaration is kept on record and the person stays a nominee. A declared winner must be revoked before the nominee can be removed.
