# Reviewing Client Conversations (Read-only)

As Department Head you can read the chat between each customer of your department and the Team Member and Team Lead who look after them, to check the quality of service. You read; you never write.

## Before you start

:::prereq
- You are a Department Head. You see only customers of your own department. The Super Admin can review every department.
- You are not a participant of these conversations. The customer is never shown your name, and nothing you do appears in the conversation.
:::

## Steps

1. Click **Chat** in the menu (in the **Team** section), then the **Client conversations** tab.
2. Find the customer in the list. You can search by customer name or customer code. Only conversations that have at least one message are listed, newest first. Click **Show more** if the list is long.
3. Click the customer. The conversation opens with the names of the customer's Team Member and Team Lead at the top and a **Review only** marker.

## What you should see

:::result
The conversation is shown with each sender's name and role (**Client**, **Team Member**, **Team Lead**). There is no message box and no send button. A note at the bottom says: "Review only. You can read this conversation but not write in it. Opening it is recorded in the audit log." New messages appear on their own.
:::

## Good to know

:::important Reading is recorded
Opening a client conversation is written to the audit log with your name, the customer and the time. If you open the same conversation again within 30 minutes, it is not recorded a second time.
:::

- Reading is enforced by the system, not only by the screen. A Department Head cannot send a message or upload a file to a client conversation by any route.
- If the customer is reassigned, you still see the whole conversation, because it belongs to the customer.
- Internal chats between employees are not visible to you unless you are a member of them.
- To speak to the customer, ask the Team Member or Team Lead, or use your own team chat with them (see [Chat](/shared/chat-guide)).

## If something goes wrong

- **The tab is missing**: only Department Heads and the Super Admin have it. If you are a Department Head and it is missing, contact your Super Admin.
- **A customer is not listed**: it has no messages yet, or it belongs to another department.
