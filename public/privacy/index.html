Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Amuwa Corporation ("Amuwa", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information in the Amuwa CRM at crm.amuwacorporation.com (the "CRM"), including its client portal, meeting booking pages and the related e-mails. The CRM is a business tool used by our employees to work with our clients, leads and job candidates.

1. Who this policy covers

  • Employees and staff who sign in to the CRM.
  • Clients and their contacts who use the client portal or whose business details we manage.
  • Leads, guests and candidates who book a meeting, are invited to one, or whose details are shared with us.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, work e-mail address, phone number, role, department and sign-in records.
  • Business records: client and lead details, quotations, invoices, payments, documents, tasks, tickets, messages and notes entered by our staff or clients.
  • Employee records (for staff only): attendance, leave, HR documents and performance information, as needed to run the company.
  • Meeting information: meeting title, time, attendees, guest names and e-mail addresses, and the meeting link.
  • Technical and security information: IP address, approximate location derived from it, browser type, and an audit log of important actions. We use these to keep accounts secure and to prevent abuse.
  • Google user data, only if you choose to connect your Google Calendar (see section 4).

3. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the CRM and the client portal.
  • To schedule meetings, send invitations, confirmations and reminders.
  • To send service e-mails, such as sign-in links, password resets and notifications about your records.
  • To keep the CRM secure: detect fraud, abuse and unauthorised access, and keep an audit trail.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Google user data (Google Calendar)

Connecting a Google account is optional. When an employee clicks Connect Google Calendar, Google asks them to allow the following access. We request only what the meetings feature needs:

  • Your Google account e-mail address (openid, email): to show which Google account is connected.
  • Calendar events (calendar.events): to create, update and cancel the events for meetings booked in the CRM on your calendar, including a Google Meet link when requested. We do not read, change or delete any other events in your calendar.
  • Free / busy information (calendar.freebusy): to see when you are busy, so that colleagues and guests are offered only free times. We see busy time ranges only, never the titles, attendees or details of your other events.

How Google user data is stored and protected

We store the connected Google e-mail address, the access tokens Google gives us, and the identifiers of the calendar events the CRM created. Tokens are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before they are stored, and the key is held separately from the database. Busy times are fetched when needed and are not stored.

Sharing of Google user data

We do not share, sell or transfer Google user data to anyone, except where needed to provide the meetings feature to you (for example, a meeting's time and attendees are visible to its invited participants), where required by law, or with your consent.

Limited Use

Amuwa's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, Google user data is not used for advertising, is not used to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models, and is not read by people except with your permission, for security purposes, or where required by law.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect at any time from Meetings → Google, hours & booking links in the CRM, or by removing access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When you disconnect in the CRM, we revoke the access at Google and delete your stored Google tokens.

5. Service providers

We use trusted providers who process data on our behalf, only to run the CRM:

  • Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in).
  • Cloudflare and Vercel (hosting and network security).
  • Google (Calendar and Meet, for those who connect them, and e-mail delivery).
  • Amazon Web Services (e-mail delivery).

These providers may store data outside India, with appropriate safeguards. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights, users or the public.

6. Data retention

We keep information for as long as it is needed for the purposes above. Business, tax and accounting records are kept for the periods the law requires. Security logs are kept for a limited time. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

7. Security

We protect information with encrypted connections (HTTPS), encryption of sensitive secrets, role-based access so people see only what their role allows, sign-in protection and rate limits, and audit logs. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will act promptly on any incident.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, withdraw consent where we rely on it, and raise a grievance with us. Contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity first.

9. Children

The CRM is a business tool and is not intended for children under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be announced in the CRM.
